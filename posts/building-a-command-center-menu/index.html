Building a Command Center Menu

I honestly can’t sleep if my ideas or concepts aren’t out of my brain yet…

Overhauling a website menu shouldn’t feel like navigating a boring spreadsheet, but standard nav bars always play it way too safe. For the latest update to iamchrispaezjr.com, I wanted something that actually had a pulse — so I turned a basic drop-down into a full-on dashboard.

Inspired by TagDiv’s clean newspaper layouts, I mashed everything together into one unified menu modal. At the very top, I dropped in a social link scroller complete with active follower counters just for the socials, keeping the rest of the navigational links clean and out of the way. It gives the whole site less of a standard portfolio vibe and more of an interactive command center.
