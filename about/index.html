About Me

I'm a Public Figure, Digital Creator & Entrepreneur.

I operate with the mindset and ambition of a full-scale media company. I use video editing tools, VFX, animation, and AI to transform a deep appreciation for media and entertainment into originally produced content. I use my experience in working with 2D and 3D animation, visual effects, and full-scale content creation to bring new and original ideas and IPs to life for social media and other platforms.

Truth is, I’m not just trying to make silly videos on the internet. I’m chasing the absolute limits of my creativity and imagination. For me, storytelling, world-building, and escaping the confines of reality are what make me love pop-culture so much. I'm eventually aiming to dive into full-scale world-building—whether that's short films, animated series, books, comics, apps, games, music, AR/VR, or interactive media. It’s all about telling damn good stories, building entire universes, and most importantly, securing total creative and structural ownership over my content, my projects, and my audience.

I’m self-taught in tools like Procreate, Blender, Adobe After Effects, Toon Boom Animate, and the Adobe Creative Suite, which I use to create, animate, edit, and add effects while keeping the story and visuals at the center. I’ve always operated by wanting to go above and beyond what’s expected of me, refusing to box myself into standard internet creator tropes. And while the craft is everything, I’m also ready to be completely transparent: I want to build a sustainable, profitable life out of this.

As a big pop-culture fan, some of my biggest inspirations are Star Wars, Indiana Jones, Back To The Future, The Avengers, WandaVision, The Flash, Wizards of (and beyond) Waverly Place, The Last of Us, Star Wars Battlefront 2, Harry Potter, Full Metal Alchemist, AppleTV's Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed, and my current favorite show, Silo!

I’m open to collaborating or freelancing with other creatives, teams, or businesses that share these exact interests, or exploring long-term, meaningful employment where I can build cool things at scale.
