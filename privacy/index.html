Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when visiting our website and subscribing to our mailing list.

2. Information We Collect

Email Subscribers: We collect your email address when you voluntarily sign up for our newsletter via our website. This data is processed and stored by Mailchimp. We use this information to send you updates, newsletters, and other content you have requested.

Automatically Collected Information: Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain technical information about your visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, through standard tracking technologies (e.g., cookies) for analytical purposes.

3. Advertising

We may in the future display advertisements on this website. These advertisements may be served by third-party advertising partners who may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your interactions with these ads and other websites to provide targeted advertising based on your interests.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve our website.
  • Send newsletters and requested communications.
  • Monitor and analyze website usage and trends.
  • Deliver relevant advertisements (when applicable).

5. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell your personal information. We share your email address with Mailchimp as our email service provider. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in any email we send. You may also contact us to request the deletion of your personal data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us.
