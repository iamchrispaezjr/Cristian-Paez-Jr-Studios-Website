Ways to work together

  • Brand partnership Sponsored posts, shoutouts, and longer collaborations across social platforms.
  • Sponsored video Dedicated or integrated YouTube / TikTok content featuring your product or brand.
  • Display / banner Static image or HTML ad units on web projects and other properties — a fixed placement on a page.
  • In-feed Promoted posts and ad units that appear inside social feeds as people scroll.
  • UGC content Custom user-generated-style creatives built with VFX, animation, AI, or all three — ready for ads, social, or product pages.
  • Product-for-post Value-based collabs centered on receiving product (merch, gear, tech) in exchange for content, sometimes with a small baseline fee for production or shipping.
