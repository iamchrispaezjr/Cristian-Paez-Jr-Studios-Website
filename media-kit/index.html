Ad slots, handshake deals & affiliates

  • Display / banner A fixed ad unit on web projects and other properties — buy the slot by the month.
  • In-feed slot An ad unit that sits in a social feed as people scroll. You’re buying the placement, not a custom endorsement.
  • Handshake swap I place your ad on my stuff, you place my ad on yours. Equal inventory, no cash required unless the slots don’t match.
  • Affiliate links I’m open to affiliate partnerships — a tracked link on my properties, with commission on sales or sign-ups. Ask if you have a program that fits.
  • Multi-slot package One buy across more than one placement — same creative, same window, quoted as a package.
  • UGC ad creative If you need the actual ad made, I can deliver UGC-style spots with VFX, animation, AI, or all three.
  • Custom inventory If the slot you want isn’t listed, ask. I’ll tell you if I have the space and what it costs.
