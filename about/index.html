About Me

Creator, tinkerer, and pop-culture nerd taking it one idea at a time.

Visual Effects Animation Web Design Interior Design UI Apple / Tech

Hey! I’m Chris.

I haven’t exactly landed on what I’d like to do in life… so I’m taking it one step at a time.

I have a love for Visual Effects, Animation, Web Design, Interior Design, and so much more.

I have a passion for seamless UI integration, being irritated and annoyed by complicated user interfaces that confuse visitors and make it harder to complete an objective (such as Facebook’s crappy UI for deactivating accounts).

I love pop-culture media and entertainment, escaping into other worlds and stories that allow me to live out another reality, that isn’t mundane like our own.

I love popular movies, television shows, books & video-games like The Last Of Us, The Avengers, Marvel, Harry Potter, Star Wars: Battlefront 2, Wizards of (and beyond) Waverly Place, and AppleTV’s prestigious shows, Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed, and my new favorite, Silo!

And I really love technology, especially Apple’s products that are designed very minimalistic, while still retaining some of that artistic edge that other technology companies do not have.

So what exactly am I working on…? Honestly, I’m not sure. I have too many ideas, inspirations, and passions to completely fill out one box, so for now, I’m taking it one step at a time, putting stuff out there, And hoping that my audience, finds inspiration as I have from others 😌.
