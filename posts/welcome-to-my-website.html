Welcome to My Website

My name is Cristian Paez Jr, and I’m a digital creator and entrepreneur.

Hello There! If you’ve poked around the internet looking for creators lately, you might've seen most popular creators use normal plug and play options, link-in-bio tools, or bulky e-commerce platforms For a long time, those were my home base too. I originally had my website and my digital footprint using tools like Beacons.ai, Squarespace, and Shopify. They get the job done on paper, but I quickly realized I had a recurring problem. Subconsciously, I just hated how they looked. No matter how much I tweaked the templates, adjusted sections, or edited the website they never felt truly mine and it felt super ugly. I hated having to abide by the rules of the webhosts and how THEY structured their templates.

So, I decided to take matters into my own hands. I ditched the rigid templates and coded my own website from scratch. Building it from the ground up gave me total creative freedom to design an experience that actually reflects who I am and what I do without abiding by the web hosts rules.

Moving forward, this site is going to be a living, breathing hub. I plan to regularly push site updates as well as deep-dive project updates right here in my blog. If you want a transparent look at what I’m working on behind the scenes, this is where you’ll find it—and I genuinely hope you’ll stick around to read along as things evolve.

There is plenty more on the way, so please consider subscribing to my newsletter to stay in the loop. While you’re here, be sure to check out my store, follow me at my socials, and explore my website and projects while you’re here.

See you soon!.
